Bachelor of Science in Office Administration (BSOA)

Empowering the next generation of organizational leaders, communication experts, and administrative professionals.

Why Office Administration Matters

The Bachelor of Science in Office Administration is a comprehensive program that focuses on corporate office management, business communication, advanced technology, and vital administrative skills needed in today's diverse workplaces.

The Core Impact: BSOA is the backbone of business efficiency. It ensures organizations remain highly organized, productive, and agile while fostering seamless communication and smooth day-to-day operations.

  • Records Management
  • Business Communication
  • Computer Applications
  • Customer Service
  • Administrative Management
